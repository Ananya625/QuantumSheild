Traditional Algorithms (RSA / ECC)
Traditional cryptographic methods rely on mathematical problems like integer factorization (RSA) or discrete logarithms (ECC/DH). A large-scale quantum computer using Shor's algorithm can solve these problems in polynomial time, exposing historical sessions.
Lattice-Based Security (ML-KEM)
ML-KEM (standardized by NIST in FIPS 203) is built on the hardness of the Module Learning-With-Errors (MLWE) problem. There are no known classical or quantum algorithms that can break this structure in polynomial time, securing data even after a quantum jump.
